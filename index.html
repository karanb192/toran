Toran

A toran hangs over the door on festival days, strung with marigolds and mango leaves, sometimes beads and small bells. This one hangs in your browser. Brush it and the bells ring. It sways with the wind where you are, from live weather data.

Tap a leaf to read a door someone remembers. Add a line about yours. Each accepted line hangs on a leaf with a yellow knot.

Got a detail wrong about your region's toran? Suggest a fix.

There is one secret.

Inspired by Chimes by Marina Budarina, a beaded curtain for every country. The strings use Verlet physics, the approach in Liam Egan's CodePen that Chimes builds on. Wind from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Made by Karan Bansal. Code under MIT.
